## Ingredients
#buy
- [x] ½ cup tahini
- [x] ¼ cup water (or more for a thinner consistency)
- [x] ¼ cup lemon juice (about 2 lemons)
- [x] 2 garlic cloves (minced)
- [x] ½ teaspoon sea salt
- [x] ¼ teaspoon cumin

## Directions
1. Add tahini, water, lemon juice, garlic, salt, and cumin to a mixing bowl.
2. Whisk until creamy, adding more water if needed.
3. Taste and adjust seasoning to your preference.
## Flavour variations
• **Green Tahini Sauce:** Blend tahini with green herbs (parsley, cilantro, dill, or mint).
• **Spicy Tahini Sauce:** Add cayenne, chili powder, za’atar, or blend with jalapeño or serrano pepper for heat.
• **Garlic Tahini Sauce:** Add more minced garlic or roast garlic for a milder flavor before blending with tahini.
