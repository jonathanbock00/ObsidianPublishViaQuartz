# Ingredients
#buy
- [x] 500 g chickpeas (soaked for at least 24 hours, rinsed well)
- [x] 300 g chopped onions
- [x] 14 g chopped garlic
- [x] 60 g olive oil
- [x] 3 heaped tsp oregano
- [x] 0.75 tsp black pepper
- [x] 2 tsp salt
- [x] Water (enough to cover chickpeas by 1 cm)
- [x] Juice from 1 large lemon
- [x] 15 g olive oil

# Directions
1. Rinse chickpeas well, then cook for 10-15 minutes, skimming off any foam.
2. In a second pot, sauté onions in half the olive oil for 5 minutes until translucent and slightly golden.
3. Add garlic, oregano, black pepper, and salt, and sauté for another 2 minutes, stirring.
4. Add rinsed and precooked chickpeas to the pot.
5. Pour in water until it covers the chickpeas by 1 cm.
6. Cook for 1 hour, skimming foam as needed.
7. After 30 minutes of cooking, adjust seasoning with additional salt or spices if desired.
8. Once cooked, stir in the remaining olive oil and fresh lemon juice.
9. Serve warm, ensuring chickpeas are just covered with liquid for a creamy consistency.

## Possible Improvements
Here are a few ways to elevate the original Greek-style chickpea soup (Revithia) recipe while staying true to its authentic flavors, based on comparisons with other trusted versions:

**1. Use Fresh Herbs**

- Many authentic recipes incorporate fresh rosemary, bay leaves, or thyme for a more aromatic base.

**Improvement:** Add a sprig of fresh rosemary or 1-2 bay leaves during the simmering phase.

**2. Enhance the Flavor with Stock**

- While water is traditional, using vegetable or chicken stock can deepen the flavor.

**Improvement:** Substitute part of the water with a light vegetable stock.

**3. Achieve Creaminess Without Over-Thickening**

- Some Greek chefs mash a small portion of the cooked chickpeas and stir them back into the soup to create a creamier texture.

**Improvement:** Mash about ½ cup of the chickpeas after cooking and mix them into the soup.

**4. Low and Slow Cooking for Depth**

- Authentic Revithia is often cooked very slowly to develop rich flavors. Some recipes even recommend slow-cooking overnight.

**Improvement:** Simmer the soup on low heat for 2-3 hours if possible.

**5. Experiment with Olive Oil and Lemon**

- High-quality Greek olive oil and fresh lemon juice are crucial for this dish. Adding a final drizzle of olive oil and a squeeze of lemon before serving enhances the dish.

**Improvement:** Use the best extra virgin olive oil you can find, and reserve some for garnish.

**6. Incorporate Caramelized Onions**

- Caramelizing the onions adds natural sweetness and depth.

**Improvement:** Cook the onions slowly until golden and sweet before adding the chickpeas.

**7. Serve with Traditional Accompaniments**

- Serving Revithia with crusty bread or olives complements the flavors.

**Improvement:** Pair the soup with fresh, warm pita or kalamata olives for authenticity.

**8. Spices for Warmth**

- Some regional recipes add a hint of ground cumin or paprika for warmth and complexity.

**Improvement:** Add ½ tsp of ground cumin or sweet paprika during the sautéing stage.

**Final Touch**

Finish the dish with a garnish of fresh parsley or dill for a fresh, vibrant note.

These enhancements balance authenticity with an elevated flavor profile while respecting the simplicity and tradition of Revithia.
