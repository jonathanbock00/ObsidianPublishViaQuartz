# Directions
1. Heat oil in a pot, cook onions for 1-2 minutes until translucent.
	- [ ] 2 tbsp olive oil
	- [ ] 1 medium onion diced
	- [ ] 3 cloves garlic minced
2. Add ginger and garlic, cook for another minute.
	- [ ] 3 tbsp ginger minced or finely diced
	- [ ] 3 cloves garlic minced
3. Add carrots, stir, and cook for 10 minutes.
	- [ ] 2 lbs carrots peeled and chopped
4. Add broth, bay leaf, cinnamon, and salt. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer for 20-30 minutes until carrots are soft.
	- [ ] 4 cups vegetable broth
	- [ ] 1 bay leaf
	- [ ] 1 tsp cinnamon
	- [ ] 1 tsp salt
5. Blend soup until smooth. Remove bay leaf. Serve with optional toppings.
	- [ ] 1 bay leaf (remove)
