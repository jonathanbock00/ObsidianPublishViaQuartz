# Ingredients
#buy
- [x] 1 tbsp coconut oil
- [x] 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- [x] 4 garlic cloves, minced
- [x] 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated and peeled
- [x] 2 (15.5 oz) cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- [x] 1 (13.5 oz) can full-fat coconut milk
- [x] 1 cup low-sodium vegetable broth
- [x] 2 tbsp curry powder (or more to taste)
- [x] 1½ tsp garam masala
- [x] 1 tsp ground cumin
- [x] 1 tsp kosher salt
- [x] 1 bunch cilantro, chopped (plus more for garnish)
- [x] 1 lime, juiced (about 2 tbsp)

#### For Serving
- cooked rice
- lime slices

# Directions
1. Heat oil in a large skillet. Sauté onion for 4–5 minutes until softened.
2. Add garlic and ginger; stir for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Stir in chickpeas, coconut milk, broth, curry powder, garam masala, cumin, and salt. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer for 10 minutes.
4. Turn off heat; stir in cilantro and lime juice.
5. Serve over rice, garnished with more cilantro and lime wedges.
