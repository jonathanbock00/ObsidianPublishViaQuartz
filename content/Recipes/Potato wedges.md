# Ingredients
#buy
- [x] 1 ½ teaspoons dried oregano
- [x] 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- [x] 1 teaspoon onion powder
- [x] 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- [x] ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
- [x] 2 pounds waxy potatoes (e.g., Yukon Gold)
- [x] 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- [x] Optional garnish: crumbled feta, finely chopped fresh parsley or dill

# Directions
1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). In a small bowl, mix garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper.
2. Slice potatoes into 8 wedges each. Toss with oil and seasoning in a bowl.
3. Spread wedges on a baking sheet in a single layer. Roast for 25 minutes, flip, and roast for another 15–20 minutes.
4. Serve with tzatziki sauce or alongside Greek chicken burgers.
