## Ingredients
#buy
- [x] ½ cup tahini
- [x] 3 whole roasted red peppers
- [x] ⅓ cup water
- [x] 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- [x] 1 garlic clove
- [x] salt
## Directions
1. Add tahini, red peppers, water, lemon juice, garlic, and salt to a food processor.
2. Blend until smooth, adding 1–2 tablespoons water for a thinner consistency.
3. Serve immediately or store in a sealed container.

## Notes
- Adjust flavor by adding an extra roasted red pepper for more red pepper taste.
