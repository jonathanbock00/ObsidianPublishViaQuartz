# Ingredients
#buy
- [x] ⅓ cup red wine vinegar
- [x] 1 lemon, juiced
- [x] 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- [x] 2 garlic cloves, minced
- [x] ½ teaspoon dried oregano
- [x] ¼ teaspoon kosher salt
- [x] ¼ teaspoon black pepper
- [x] ½ cup olive oil
# Directions
1. Mix red wine vinegar, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
2. Gradually whisk in olive oil until emulsified.
3. Use immediately or store in an airtight container in the refrigerator.
