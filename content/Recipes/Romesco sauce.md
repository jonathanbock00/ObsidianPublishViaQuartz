# Ingredients
#buy
- [x] ½ cup raw hazelnuts
- [x] ½ cup slivered almonds
- [x] 1 (14.5-ounce) can diced fire-roasted tomatoes, drained
- [x] 1 (12-ounce) jar roasted red peppers, drained
- [x] 3 garlic cloves, peeled
- [x] 2 tbsp red wine vinegar or sherry vinegar
- [x] 1 tsp smoked paprika
- [x] 1 tsp kosher salt
- [x] ¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil (or more for a thinner texture)

# Directions
1. Toast hazelnuts in a pan for 5–8 minutes. Cool and remove skins by rubbing.
2. Toast almonds in the same pan for 3–4 minutes until golden.
3. Blend nuts, tomatoes, peppers, garlic, vinegar, paprika, salt, and olive oil until smooth.
4. Garnish with chopped nuts or parsley and serve.
