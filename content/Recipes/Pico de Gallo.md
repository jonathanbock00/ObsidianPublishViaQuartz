## Ingredients
#buy
- [x] 4 Roma tomatoes, deseeded and diced
- [x] ⅔ cup white onion, finely diced
- [x] 1 bunch cilantro, finely chopped
- [x] 1 serrano pepper or jalapeño, finely chopped
- [x] 1 lime, juiced
- [x] ½ teaspoon salt

## Directions
1. Stir all ingredients together in a bowl.
2. Let sit for 15 minutes for the best flavor. Serve with tortilla chips.

## Notes
- Make ahead for a few hours for more robust flavor.
- Keeps for 3 days in the fridge.
