# Ingredients
#buy
- [x] 2 large avocados, chilled
- [x] ½ cup full-fat coconut milk
- [x] ⅓ cup raw cacao powder
- [x] ⅓ cup maple syrup
- [x] 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- [x] Optional toppings: chopped hazelnuts, sea salt
# Directions
1. Slice the avocados, remove the pit, and scoop out the flesh into a food processor.
2. Add the coconut milk, cacao powder, maple syrup, and vanilla extract.
3. Blend until smooth and creamy, scraping the sides as needed.
4. Taste and adjust sweetness if necessary.
5. Scoop into bowls and garnish with hazelnuts and sea salt before serving.
