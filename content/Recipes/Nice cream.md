# Ingredients
#buy
- [x] 2 frozen bananas
- [x] 1 cup frozen fruit of choice (e.g. mango, berries, pineapple)
- [x] 1–2 tbsp plant milk (optional, for blending)
# Directions
1. Add frozen bananas and chosen fruit to a high-speed blender or food processor.
2. Blend until smooth and creamy, adding a splash of plant milk if needed.
3. Serve immediately as soft-serve or freeze for 1–2 hours for a firmer texture.
