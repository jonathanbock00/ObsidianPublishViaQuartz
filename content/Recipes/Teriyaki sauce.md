# Ingredients
#buy
- [x] 250 ml light soy sauce
- [x] 200 ml mirin
- [x] 200 ml sake
- [x] 60-90 g sugar

# Directions
1. Combine the soy sauce, mirin, sake, and sugar in a pan over low heat until the sugar dissolves.
2. For a dipping sauce, cook longer until the mixture thickens.
