# Ingredients
#buy
- [x] 2 tablespoons cacao powder
- [x] 2 tablespoons maple syrup
- [x] 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- [x] 1 cup milk (dairy or dairy-free)
- [x] ¼ cup chia seeds
- [x] Garnishes: raspberries, chocolate shavings, coconut whipped cream, nuts, and seeds

# Directions
1. Whisk together cacao powder, maple syrup, vanilla extract, milk, and chia seeds in a bowl.
2. Let the mixture sit for 15 minutes, then whisk again. Cover and refrigerate for 4 hours or overnight.
3. Stir and serve in small glasses. Garnish with fruit, chocolate, or whipped cream.
