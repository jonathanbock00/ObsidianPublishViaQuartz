# Ingredients  
#buy  
- [x] 2 cups mixed salad greens (e.g., spinach, arugula, or lettuce)  
- [x] 1 ripe avocado, diced  
- [x] tomatoes, cubed
- [x] 1/3 cup cucumber, diced  
- [x] 1/3 cup roasted chickpeas or a vegan protein alternative like grilled tofu or tempeh or vegan chicken nuggets
- [x] 2 tablespoons ajvar
- [x] 2 tablespoons olive oil  
- [x] 1 tablespoon lemon juice  
- [x] 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder  
- [x] Salt and pepper to taste  
- [x] Optional: pumpkin seeds or sunflower seeds for crunch  

# Directions  
1. **Prepare the Salad Base**: Combine the salad greens, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, and avocado in a large bowl.  
2. **Cook Vegan Protein**: If using tofu or tempeh, grill or sauté until golden and slightly crisp. If using roasted chickpeas, ensure they are pre-seasoned or flavored. Add the protein to the salad.  
3. **Mix the Dressing**: In a small bowl, whisk together ajvar, olive oil, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until smooth.  
4. **Assemble and Serve**: Drizzle the dressing over the salad and toss gently to combine. Top with optional seeds for added crunch and enjoy immediately.  

This salad is perfect as a main dish or a side and can be adapted with seasonal vegetables!
