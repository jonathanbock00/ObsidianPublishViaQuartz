# Ingredients
#buy
- [x] 1 block silken tofu (300g)
- [x] 2 tbsp oil
- [x] 1 onion, diced
- [x] 4 cloves garlic, minced
- [x] 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
- [x] 2 tbsp lemon juice
- [x] 1 tsp miso paste
- [x] 1/2 tsp salt (divided)
- [x] More salt and pepper to taste

# Directions
1. Cook pasta according to package instructions, reserving 1/2 cup pasta water.
2. Sauté diced onion in oil for 1 minute, add garlic, and cook for another 30 seconds.
3. Blend tofu, sautéed onion, garlic, lemon juice, nutritional yeast, and miso until smooth.
4. Gradually add reserved pasta water to achieve a creamy texture.
5. Toss cooked pasta with tofu sauce. Adjust thickness with more pasta water if needed.
6. Season with salt and pepper, and garnish with parsley or vegan parmesan (optional).
