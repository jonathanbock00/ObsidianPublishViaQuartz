# Ingredients
#buy
- [x] 1 tablespoon olive oil
- [x] 1 small onion diced
- [x] 2 garlic cloves minced
- [x] ½ red bell pepper diced
- [x] ½ teaspoon chili powder
- [x] 1 cup long grain white rice
- [x] ½ teaspoon cumin
- [x] kosher salt to taste
- [x] 8 ounces tomato sauce
- [x] 1 cup vegetable or chicken broth

# Directions
1. Wash and drain the rice in a fine mesh sieve.
2. Heat oil in a saucepan, sauté onions, garlic, bell pepper, and chili powder for 1-2 minutes.
3. Add rice, cook until golden, then add cumin, salt, tomato sauce, and broth.
4. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce heat, and simmer for 15 minutes.
5. Fluff rice with a fork and serve.
