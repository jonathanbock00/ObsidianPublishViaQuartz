# Ingredients
#buy
- [x] ½ cup finely chopped flat-leaf parsley leaves
- [x] 1 tbsp finely chopped fresh oregano leaves or 1 tsp dried oregano
- [x] 1 tbsp finely chopped red chili pepper or ½ tsp dried red pepper flakes
- [x] 3–4 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- [x] ½ cup extra virgin olive oil
- [x] 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- [x] ½ tsp kosher salt
- [x] ¼ tsp freshly ground black pepper

# Directions
1. Finely chop parsley, oregano, chili pepper, and garlic. Alternatively, pulse in a food processor.
2. Combine chopped ingredients in a bowl.
3. Add olive oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper; stir to mix.
4. Serve immediately or refrigerate for later use.
