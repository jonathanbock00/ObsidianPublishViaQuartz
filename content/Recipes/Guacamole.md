## Ingredients
#buy
- [x] 3 ripe avocados
- [x] ½ small yellow onion, finely diced
- [x] 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
- [x] 3 tablespoons finely chopped fresh cilantro
- [x] 1 jalapeno pepper, seeds removed and finely diced
- [x] 2 garlic cloves, minced
- [x] 1 lime, juiced
- [x] ½ teaspoon sea salt
## Directions
1. Slice avocados, remove the pit, and scoop into a bowl.
2. Mash the avocado to desired consistency.
3. Add remaining ingredients and stir together. Taste and adjust salt or lime juice if needed.
4. Serve with tortilla chips.
