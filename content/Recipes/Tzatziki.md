# Ingredients
#buy
- [x] 1 ½ cups Greek yogurt
- [x] 1 medium cucumber, peeled and grated
- [x] 2 tablespoons fresh dill, chopped
- [x] 2 cloves garlic, minced
- [x] 2 tablespoons olive oil
- [x] 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
- [x] ½ teaspoon kosher salt

# Directions
1. Drain the grated cucumber by pressing it over a sieve or using a nut milk bag.
2. Combine all ingredients in a bowl and mix well.
3. Let rest for a few minutes and adjust seasoning if needed.
4. Serve immediately or refrigerate for up to 4 days.

# Notes
- This recipe makes about 2 cups of tzatziki.
