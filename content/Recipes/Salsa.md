## Ingredients

- [x] 2 cloves garlic
- [x] 1/2 yellow onion, roughly chopped
- [x] 1 jalapeno pepper, seeded and roughly chopped
- [x] 2 (14-ounce) cans fire-roasted diced tomatoes, drained
- [x] 1 (4-ounce) can green chilies
- [x] 1/2 cup fresh cilantro (about 1/2 a bunch)
- [x] 1/2 lime, juiced
- [x] 1/2 teaspoon salt (to taste)

## Directions

1. Add onion, garlic, and jalapeno to a food processor and chop finely.
2. Add tomatoes, green chilies, cilantro, lime juice, and salt.
3. Pulse in 1-second increments until well-blended but slightly chunky.
4. Adjust seasoning with salt or lime juice as needed. Serve immediately or store for later.

## Notes

- Store leftovers in glass jars.
